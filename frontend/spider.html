    Test earphones

    Rising two-note tone = locked onto him. Falling tone = lost him: stop and reset.

    Camera

    Ultra Wide is picked automatically when available. At punching range the normal lens can't fit his shoulders and arms.

    Detection

    A cue needs his elbow up at shoulder height (or fist above his head) AND the fist moving at least this fast. 3.5 is tuned on real footage.

    One swing gives one cue. A combo with his other hand can fire again 250 ms later.

    While you duck or turn, cues still fire but need a harder, faster swing, so your own movement doesn't trigger them.

    Voice

    DUCK = hook or overhand at your head. STEP BACK = straight punch down the middle. The word is louder in the ear on the side the punch comes from.

    Clips are already 1.5x faster than normal speech. Going above 1.0 here also raises the pitch.
